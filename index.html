The Nervous System

Welcome to our website about the Nervous System. The nervous system is one of the most important systems in the human body because it controls movement, emotions, memory, thoughts, and reactions. It is composed of the brain, spinal cord, and nerves that send electrical messages throughout the body. Thanks to this system, humans can communicate, feel pain, react to danger, and process information every second.

In this project, we explain how the nervous system works, the main diseases that can affect it, and how robotics and technology may improve treatments in the future. We also created a robotic solution inspired by nanotechnology that could help repair damaged nerve cells.

Research Data

The nervous system is divided into two major parts: the central nervous system and the peripheral nervous system. The central nervous system includes the brain and spinal cord, while the peripheral nervous system connects the rest of the body to the brain.

Neurons are specialized cells that transmit electrical signals through the body. The brain controls important functions such as breathing, heartbeat, emotions, and learning. Maintaining healthy habits is important because the nervous system is very delicate and can be damaged easily.

Problems

Many diseases can affect the nervous system. Some common disorders include Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease, epilepsy, and multiple sclerosis. These conditions can damage the brain, nerves, or spinal cord and affect memory, movement, and communication.

Robotic Solution

Our robotic solution is a nano robot designed to travel through the nervous system and repair damaged neurons. It could detect injured nerve cells and help regenerate nerve connections using advanced technology.
